Write {n_stories} different stories based on the following premise.

Topic: {topic}
Each story should follow a character who is feeling {emotion}.

Format:

[story 1]
<story text>

[story 2]
<story text>

[story 3]
<story text>

...

Output format requirements:

- Output exactly {n_stories} stories.
- Begin with [story 1] on its own line.
- Continue sequentially until [story {n_stories}].
- Put a blank line after each story label.
- Do not include any text before [story 1].
- Do not include any text after the final story.
- Each story must be self-contained prose with no continuity between stories.

Guidelines:

- Keep each story grounded in the topic.
- Vary phrasing and situations across stories.

IMPORTANT: You must NEVER use the word '{emotion}' or any direct synonyms of it in any story. Instead, convey the emotion ONLY through:

- The character's actions and behaviors
- Physical sensations and body language
- Dialogue and tone of voice
- Thoughts and internal reactions
- Situational context and environmental descriptions

The emotion should be clearly conveyed through these indirect means, but never explicitly named. The emotional tone should remain consistent within each story.
